What the numbers mean
Why there is no list of classes
A new class starts every 10 minutes, so there are about 66 a day, mostly with one or two people in each. Instead, everyone is grouped by the day (or the hour of the day) they signed up, and followed to the end from there.
By day / By hour
By day: one row per New York calendar day. By hour: one row per hour of the day, added up over the whole period — so with “7 days” the 10 am row is everyone who signed up between 10:00 and 10:59 on any of those days. Ad spend by hour comes straight from Meta.
Visitors
Different people who opened ridleyacademy.net/class (each browser once per row). “From ads” = arrived from a Facebook or Instagram ad.
Signed up
Registered for the evergreen class. Test sign-ups are left out. People who book again from our own no-show messages count again on the day they rebook.
Came to the class
Was in the class for at least a moment. Known about 45 minutes after their class ends (a class runs about 2½ hours), so the last few hours always show as “class not finished yet”.
Saw the offer
Was in the room after minute 75, when Stephen starts the offer — the same rule AXL uses for its Saw Offer stage. Recorded the moment it happens, and again at the end of the class for anyone who left and came back.
Sales
Sales in the Sales Log from someone who signed up here, on or after the day they signed up (rebills left out). Matched by email, which we learn when their class roster arrives.
Costs
Ad spend of the evergreen campaign (T3) divided by each step. A cost going down is green.
Arrows ▲ ▼
Compared with the same number of days just before this period (Today against yesterday, 7 days against the 7 days before). Green means better for us.
