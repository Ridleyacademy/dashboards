What the numbers mean
Registered
Signed up on ridleyacademy.net/register for this webinar.
Showed up
Joined the live room at least once.
Saw the offer
Stayed until the offer starts. That is minute 90 unless it was changed for that webinar — use “✎ Change” on the Saw the offer step when the offer started earlier or later.
Sales
Sales in the Sales Log from people linked to this webinar. People who attended count for 30 days afterwards (so follow-up and phone-closed deals land here); people who registered but didn’t show count until the next webinar starts; anyone with no registration counts only if the sale happened during the live event. Rebills, refunds and chargebacks are left out.
Live / Rep
Live = the sale happened during the webinar with nobody closing it. Rep = a sales rep is credited (Affiliate column, their declaration, or a call of 3+ minutes).
Paid in full / Payment plan
How the customer paid, from the Sales Log status.
Revenue
What customers paid, before anything is taken off.
Revenue after fees
Revenue minus rep commission (10%), rep manager commission (5%), card processor fees and the buy-now-pay-later cost (20%).
Profit
Revenue after fees minus ad spend and text-message cost.
$ back per $1
Revenue ÷ ad spend (ROAS). Above $1 means the ads paid for themselves.
Return on cost
Profit ÷ everything the webinar cost (ROI). 100% means every $1 spent came back with $1 on top.
Talked to
A rep spoke with the person for 3 minutes or more on one call. Shorter calls (voicemail, no answer, a quick hang-up) count as called, not talked to.
Time to first call
How long until a rep first called. For leads handed to reps the clock starts at the hand-over; for other offer viewers it starts when the webinar ends.
Arrows ▲ ▼
Every number is compared with the previous webinar. Green means better for us, orange means worse — so a cost going down is green.

Attendance and sales update automatically about 45 minutes after each webinar ends. Registrations come from the AXL page scenario (ridleyacademy.net/register); source tags are reported by the thank-you page. No personal data is stored or shown here. Generated —.
