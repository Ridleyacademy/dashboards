What the numbers mean
Week
Thursday to Wednesday, named by the Wednesday it ends on. Months start on the 1st. The period still running is hidden unless Current week is on, so a half-finished week doesn't look like a drop.
Arrow and colour
▲▼ show which way the number moved since the period before. Green is good for the business, orange is bad. For “lower is better” stats (refunds, resigns, reply time) a drop is green. Percentages change in points (pts).
The line
Each point is one week (or month). The line turns red where it moved the wrong way. A dashed line is a stat typed in by hand; a solid line is counted automatically.
Automatic and typed in
Automatic stats are counted from the CRM, the Sales Log and the boards. Typed stats are entered on Enter stats. A value typed for an automatic stat replaces the automatic count for that week.
GI
Gross income — money from sales before refunds and fees.
Staff Meeting
The set of stats read out at the weekly Staff Meeting.
